The BNB contribution is determined by the actual presale result and must not exceed available proceeds after approved costs and reserves. At the full $20,000 hard cap, the target is $15,000 on the BNB side and approximately 50,000,000 FLOOR4 on the token side. No fixed external top-up is required by the approved model. The DEX, pool type, lock provider and final execution details remain to be finalized. LP creation is a separate post-presale process; no automatic LP creation in the presale contract is approved.

The $0.00030 target pool price is not a promise that the market will trade at that price. Actual pool depth, price impact and market price depend on the final pool and trading activity.
Charity commitment

Two separate project contributions

0.4% of net realized project income

Calculated after direct project costs. The exact accounting methodology for taxes, refunds and shared costs must be documented before the first accounting period.

0.4% of the Treasury token allocation

At the current working Treasury allocation of 800,000,000 FLOOR4, this equals 3,200,000 FLOOR4. The final token quantity changes if the Treasury allocation changes. These tokens are not automatically sold.

The two 0.4% commitments have different calculation bases. They are not a 4% fee, not an automatic deduction from each transaction and not an automatic on-chain tax. The Christmas Challenge charity vote is separate and its purpose/recipient is selected by the community.
