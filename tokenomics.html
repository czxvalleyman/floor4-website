Charity commitment

Two separate project contributions

0.4% of net realized project income

Calculated on income actually realized by the project after direct project costs. It is not 0.4% of gross presale proceeds or an automatic deduction from each transaction.

0.4% of the Treasury token allocation

This is a separate token allocation carved out of the Treasury allocation. At the current working Treasury allocation of 800,000,000 FLOOR4, it corresponds to 3,200,000 FLOOR4. The final token quantity depends on the final Treasury allocation. It does not increase total supply.

Recipients, accounting methodology and reporting procedures must be established before distributions. The Christmas Challenge charity is separate and will be selected by the community.

Utility & ecosystem

Designed around participation

Community rewards

FLOOR4 and BNB rewards for selected community activities, events and airdrops, subject to published rules and available allocations.

Games & seasonal events

Community games and seasonal experiences, including the planned Christmas Challenge. Its charity purpose is selected by the community and is separate from the project’s ongoing charity policy.

Future staking

Staking is a planned future feature. Its reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and security design are not approved. No fixed APY or staking return is promised. NFTs remain a possible future feature, not a current product commitment.
