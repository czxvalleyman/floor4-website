At the full $20,000 hard cap, the target initial pool value is approximately $30,000 in total, split between $15,000 in BNB and $15,000 worth of FLOOR4 at the target DEX price. The target LP lock is 12 months. The DEX, pool type and lock provider remain to be finalized. LP creation is a separate post-presale process; no automatic LP creation in the presale contract is approved.

The $0.00030 target pool price is not a promise that the market will trade at that price. Actual pool depth, price impact and market price depend on the final pool and trading activity.
Charity commitment

Two separate project contributions

0.4% of net realized project income

Calculated on income actually realized by the project after direct project costs. It is not 0.4% of gross presale proceeds or an automatic deduction from each transaction.

0.4% of the final Treasury token allocation

This is a separate token allocation carved out of the Treasury allocation. The final token quantity depends on the Treasury allocation approved in the final distribution. Recipients and the accounting process will be documented separately.

Utility & ecosystem

Designed around participation

Community rewards

FLOOR4 and BNB rewards for selected community activities, events and airdrops, subject to published rules and available allocations.

Games & seasonal events

Community games and seasonal experiences, including planned events such as the Christmas Challenge. Its charity vote is separate from the ongoing project charity policy above.

Future staking

Staking is a planned future development area. No APY, reward source, allocation, lock-up terms or launch date have been approved. No fixed staking return is promised.
