Floor 05 · Development path

ROADMAP

One level at a time. Follow the work behind FLOOR 4 — from the foundations to a growing community ecosystem.

This is a public high-level roadmap. Quarters are planning windows, not guaranteed delivery dates. Seasonal events may move with operational readiness. Milestones depend on technical, security, legal, financial and community factors.
01
Q4 2026 · Foundation

Build the foundations

Establish the FLOOR 4 identity and The Missing Floor story; develop the website and core documentation; define tokenomics; prepare the community channels and technical launch requirements.

02
Q4 2026 · Presale & token launch

Open the next chapter

The planned presale runs from 24 November 2026 at 18:00 UTC to 4 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC. The approved presale allocation is 0.1125% of the planned 4,444,444,444 FLOOR4 supply, or 5,000,000 FLOOR4. The presale price has not been approved and will not be published until formally decided. BNB is the only accepted payment asset. The hard cap is $20,000 with no soft cap. The minimum purchase is $20. The maximum cumulative purchase is $400 per wallet across the entire presale. Repeat purchases are allowed within the remaining wallet limit. Presale vesting is 20% at launch and 80% linearly over the following 90 days. The target initial DEX pool price is $0.00030 per FLOOR4, not a guaranteed market price. Launch is targeted for December 2026, but no exact launch date is approved and readiness requirements still apply.

03
Q4 2026 · Christmas Challenge

A season of discovery

The planned Christmas Challenge is built around a 24-day advent calendar from 1–24 December. The concept includes daily puzzles and community activities, a mix of FLOOR4 token and BNB rewards, and a charity purpose selected by the community. The event is not represented as production-ready; it must pass the project’s security and release checks first.

04
Q1 2027 · New Year & Lunar New Year

Keep the story moving

Seasonal community activities around the New Year and Lunar New Year, with timing and format adjusted to readiness and community interest.

05
Q1–Q2 2027 · Ecosystem expansion

Build useful ways to participate

Develop community games, seasonal events and additional ecosystem experiences. Staking is a planned feature for this period; its reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and security design remain to be defined and reviewed. NFTs are a possible future ecosystem feature.

06
Target: Q2 2027 · Exchange expansion

Explore broader market access

Work toward a first additional centralized exchange listing target in Q2 2027, subject to exchange approval, eligibility, liquidity, compliance and commercial feasibility. No listing is guaranteed.

07
Q3–Q4 2027 · Long-term development

Continue building beyond the launch

Develop the FLOOR 4 community and ecosystem over time, explore partnerships and future utilities, and evolve community participation and hybrid governance. Scope and timing will follow demonstrated demand and available resources.

There is always one more floor to discover. Roadmap dates, features and milestones are indicative and may change with technical, operational, regulatory, financial or market conditions. Nothing on this page guarantees returns, exchange listings, price growth or future token value.
