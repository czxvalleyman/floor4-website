FAQ

Clear answers about the project, the token and what is still being prepared.

What is FLOOR 4?

FLOOR 4 is the project identity and token name, built around the story of The Missing Floor — a symbol of mystery, discovery and the unknown.

Which blockchain and token standard are planned?

BNB Smart Chain and the BEP-20 token standard.

What is the planned total supply?

The approved total supply for planning is 444,444,444 FLOOR4.

What are the vesting terms?

The current approved principle is 20% at launch and the remaining 80% linearly over 44 days. The detailed implementation and exact applicable allocation terms will be documented in the final sale and token documents.

How much of the presale proceeds is planned for liquidity?

The current approved principle is 60% of funds raised in the presale. Pool configuration, lock details and verification are still pending.

When is the presale planned?

The planned window is 24 November – 4 December 2026, inclusive. It remains subject to technical, security, legal and operational readiness. Final price, allocation, caps and participation instructions are not yet published.

When will the token launch?

The target is December 2026, but no specific launch date has been approved. Launch depends on technical, security, legal and financial preparations.

Is the presale open on this website?

No. This website preview does not accept token purchases. Do not send funds to wallet addresses shared through unofficial messages.

What will the token be used for?

Planned ecosystem areas include community rewards, seasonal events, airdrops, games and community activities. Staking and NFTs are future development areas; their mechanics and availability are not finalized.

Is staking available now?

No. Staking is a planned future feature. Reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and security design remain to be defined.

Will FLOOR 4 be listed on centralized exchanges?

The roadmap includes a target to explore a first additional centralized exchange in Q2 2027. No listing is guaranteed; exchanges make their own independent decisions.

Where are the official community links?

Verified links will be added when official destinations are confirmed. Be cautious of impersonators and unsolicited messages.

No. No return, price increase, liquidity, exchange listing or future token value is guaranteed. Digital assets involve significant risk, including the possible loss of all funds.
