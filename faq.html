Floor 08 · Answers & information

FAQ

Clear answers about the project, the token and what is still being prepared.

What is FLOOR 4?

FLOOR 4 is the project identity and token name, built around the story of The Missing Floor — a symbol of mystery, discovery and the unknown.

Which blockchain and token standard are planned?

BNB Smart Chain and the BEP-20 token standard.

What is the planned total supply?

The approved planned total supply is 4,444,444,444 FLOOR4.

What is the presale allocation?

5,000,000 FLOOR4, equal to 0.1125% of the planned total supply.

What are the presale price and payment terms?

The presale price has not been approved and will not be published until formally decided. BNB is the only accepted payment asset. The hard cap is $20,000 and there is no soft cap.

What are the purchase limits?

The minimum purchase is $20. The maximum cumulative purchase is $400 per wallet across the entire presale. Repeat purchases are allowed within the remaining wallet limit.

What are the vesting terms?

20% of each purchase becomes unlocked at token launch. The remaining 80% is released linearly over the following 90 days. There is no 90-day cliff.

When is the presale planned?

24 November 2026 at 18:00 UTC to 4 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC. The sale remains subject to technical, security, legal and operational readiness.

What is the target initial DEX price?

$0.00030 per FLOOR4 is the target initial pool price. It is not a guaranteed market price or a promise of returns.

What is the target initial liquidity?

The target BNB side of the initial liquidity pool is $15,000, with a 12-month LP lock target. At the $0.00030 target pool price, the corresponding token side is approximately 50,000,000 FLOOR4. The BNB contribution scales with actual presale proceeds and available funds. No fixed external top-up is required by the approved model. The DEX, pool type and lock provider remain to be finalized.

When will the token launch?

The target is December 2026, but no specific launch date has been approved. Launch depends on technical, security, legal and financial preparations.

Is the presale open on this website?

No. This website preview does not accept token purchases. Do not send BNB to wallet addresses shared through unofficial messages.

What will the token be used for?

Planned ecosystem areas include community rewards, seasonal events, airdrops, games and community activities. Staking and NFTs are future development areas; their mechanics and availability are not finalized.

What is the project’s charity contribution policy?

The project has approved two separate contributions: 0.4% of net realized project income after direct project costs, and a separate 0.4% of the Treasury token allocation. At the current working Treasury allocation of 800,000,000 FLOOR4, the latter corresponds to 3,200,000 FLOOR4. The exact token quantity depends on the final Treasury allocation. These are not fees on gross presale volume or individual transactions, and the two percentages have different calculation bases. The Christmas Challenge charity is separate and will be selected by the community.

Is there an automatic transaction burn?

An automatic transaction burn is an approved project intention. The burn rate, which transactions it applies to and any exemptions have not yet been finalized.

Is staking available now?

No. Staking is a planned future feature. Reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and launch timing remain to be defined.

Will FLOOR 4 be listed on centralized exchanges?

The roadmap includes a target to explore a first additional centralized exchange in Q2 2027. No listing is guaranteed; exchanges make their own independent decisions.

Does FLOOR 4 guarantee returns or price growth?

No. No return, price increase, liquidity, exchange listing or future token value is guaranteed. Digital assets involve significant risk, including the possible loss of all funds.
