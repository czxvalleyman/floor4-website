Floor 08 · Answers & information

FAQ

Clear answers about the project, the token and what is still being prepared.

What is FLOOR 4?

FLOOR 4 is the project identity and token name, built around the story of The Missing Floor — a symbol of mystery, discovery and the unknown.

Which blockchain and token standard are planned?

BNB Smart Chain and the BEP-20 token standard.

What is the planned total supply?

The approved planned total supply is 4,444,444,444 FLOOR4.

What is the presale allocation?

0.1125% of the planned total supply, equal to 5,000,000 FLOOR4. The allocation is approved; the full distribution outside the presale allocation remains a working proposal.

What are the presale dates?

24 November 2026 at 18:00 UTC to 4 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC (11 calendar days).

What is the presale token price?

It has not yet been approved and is not being published. No presale price should be assumed.

Which payment asset will be accepted?

BNB only. Binance-Peg USDC and other alternative payment assets are not accepted.

What are the presale limits?

The minimum purchase is $20 and the maximum cumulative purchase is $400 per wallet across the entire presale. Repeat purchases are allowed within the remaining wallet limit. The precise USD-to-BNB conversion and oracle safeguards must be finalized in the contract specification.

What are the vesting terms?

20% becomes claimable at token launch. The remaining 80% is released linearly over 90 days from the launch timestamp.

What is the target initial DEX price?

$0.00030 per FLOOR4 is the target price used when setting up the initial DEX pool. It is not a guaranteed market price or a promise of returns.

How much of the presale proceeds is planned for liquidity?

The initial liquidity contribution is based on actual presale proceeds. At the full $20,000 hard cap, the target BNB side is $15,000, paired with approximately 50,000,000 FLOOR4 at the $0.00030 target DEX price. The target LP lock is 12 months. The DEX, pool type and lock provider remain to be finalized.

When will the token launch?

The target is December 2026, but no specific launch date has been approved. Launch depends on technical, security, legal and financial preparations.

Is the presale open on this website?

No. This website preview does not accept token purchases. Do not send BNB to wallet addresses shared through unofficial messages.

What is the charity contribution policy?

The project has approved two separate contributions: 0.4% of net realized project income after direct project costs and 0.4% of the Treasury token allocation. At the current working Treasury allocation of 800,000,000 FLOOR4, the latter corresponds to 3,200,000 FLOOR4; the final amount depends on the final Treasury allocation. This is not a deduction from gross presale proceeds or every transaction. Accounting details and recipients/process will be documented separately. The Christmas Challenge charity is separate and will be selected by the community.

Is there an automatic transaction burn?

An automatic transaction burn is an approved project intention. The burn rate, which transactions it applies to and any exemptions have not yet been finalized. No specific burn percentage is being published.

Is staking available now?

No. Staking is a planned future feature. Reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and security design remain to be defined.

Will FLOOR 4 be listed on centralized exchanges?

The roadmap includes a target to explore a first additional centralized exchange in Q2 2027. No listing is guaranteed; exchanges make their own independent decisions.

Does FLOOR 4 guarantee returns or price growth?

No. No return, price increase, liquidity, exchange listing or future token value is guaranteed. Digital assets involve significant risk, including the possible loss of all funds.
