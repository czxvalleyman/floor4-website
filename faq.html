FAQ

Clear answers about the project, the token and what is still being prepared.

What is FLOOR 4?

FLOOR 4 is the project identity and token name, built around the story of The Missing Floor — a symbol of mystery, discovery and the unknown.

Which blockchain and token standard are planned?

BNB Smart Chain and the BEP-20 token standard.

What is the planned total supply?

The approved total supply for planning is 4,4,444,444,444 FLOOR4.

What are the vesting terms?

The current approved principle is 20% at launch and the remaining 80% linearly over 90 days from launch. The detailed implementation and exact applicable allocation terms will be documented in the final sale and token documents.

How much of the presale proceeds is planned for liquidity?

The initial liquidity contribution is based on actual presale proceeds. At the full $20,000 hard cap, the target BNB side is $15,000, paired with approximately 2,500,000 FLOOR4 at the $0.006 target pool price. The target LP lock is 12 months; the DEX, pool type and lock provider remain to be finalized.

When is the presale planned?

The presale runs from 24 November 2026 at 18:00 UTC to 4 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC. The approved price is $0.004 per FLOOR4, payment is BNB only, the hard cap is $20,000 and there is no soft cap. Transaction limits are 0.04–0.4 BNB; each wallet is limited to 250,000 FLOOR4 cumulatively. The sale remains subject to technical, security, legal and operational readiness.

What is the target initial DEX price?

$0.006 per FLOOR4 is the target price used when setting up the initial DEX pool. It is not a guaranteed market price or a promise of returns.

What is the target initial DEX price?

The current target is $0.006 per FLOOR4 when the initial pool is created. This is a planning target, not a guaranteed market price or return.

When will the token launch?

The target is December 2026, but no specific launch date has been approved. Launch depends on technical, security, legal and financial preparations.

Is the presale open on this website?

No. This website preview does not accept token purchases. Do not send funds to wallet addresses shared through unofficial messages.

What will the token be used for?

Planned ecosystem areas include community rewards, seasonal events, airdrops, games and community activities. Staking and NFTs are future development areas; their mechanics and availability are not finalized.

What is the charity contribution policy?

The project has approved two separate contributions: 0.4% of net realized project income and 0.4% of the final Treasury token allocation. The latter depends on the final Treasury allocation. This is not a 0.4% deduction from gross presale proceeds or every transaction. Accounting details and recipients/process will be documented separately.

Is staking available now?

No. Staking is a planned future feature. Reward source, rates, lock-up terms, allocation and security design remain to be defined.

Will FLOOR 4 be listed on centralized exchanges?

The roadmap includes a target to explore a first additional centralized exchange in Q2 2027. No listing is guaranteed; exchanges make their own independent decisions.

Where are the official community links?

Verified links will be added when official destinations are confirmed. Be cautious of impersonators and unsolicited messages.

What is the project’s charity commitment?

FLOOR 4 has adopted two separate commitments: 0.4% of net realized project income after direct project costs, and 0.4% of the Treasury token allocation. The final token amount depends on the Treasury allocation, which remains a working proposal. This is not a fee on gross presale volume or every transaction. The Christmas Challenge charity is separate and will be selected by the community.

Does FLOOR 4 guarantee returns or price growth?

No. No return, price increase, liquidity, exchange listing or future token value is guaranteed. Digital assets involve significant risk, including the possible loss of all funds.
