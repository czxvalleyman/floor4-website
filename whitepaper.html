WHITEPAPER

FLOOR 4 — THE MISSING FLOOR. A working overview of the project vision, token foundations and community ecosystem.

WORKING DRAFT · This page is a preliminary project overview, not a final technical, legal or regulatory whitepaper. It is not a public offer or investment recommendation. Unapproved parameters are identified as pending rather than estimated.
01 · Executive overview

The Missing Floor

FLOOR 4 takes its identity from a deliberate absence: the fourth floor that is missing from an otherwise familiar structure. The missing level represents the unknown, curiosity and the next discovery. It is the central narrative connecting the project, its visual identity and community experiences.

FLOOR 4 is both the project identity and the name associated with its token. The project is being developed around transparency, community participation and an evolving digital ecosystem rather than a single short-lived campaign.

02 · Vision & principles

Build in the open

The project aims to communicate confirmed decisions clearly, distinguish plans from delivered features and publish important changes through official channels. Community participation is intended to shape selected events and ecosystem initiatives.

03 · Network & token

BNB Smart Chain · BEP-20

The target network is BNB Smart Chain and the planned token standard is BEP-20. The approved planned total supply is 4,444,444,444 FLOOR4. Contract address, deployment transaction and verified source code will be published only after deployment and verification.

04 · Token distribution

Confirmed and pending terms

The confirmed presale allocation is 0.1125% of the planned 4,444,444,444 FLOOR4 supply, equal to 5,000,000 FLOOR4. The presale price has not been approved and must not be presented as a sale price. The hard cap is $20,000 with no soft cap, payment is BNB only, the minimum purchase is $20 and the cumulative wallet limit is $400 per wallet. Presale vesting is 20% at launch, followed by 80% linearly over 90 days. The distribution outside the presale allocation remains a working proposal and is not fully approved.

05 · Liquidity

Liquidity follows the actual presale result

The initial liquidity contribution is determined by the actual amount raised in the presale. At the full $20,000 hard cap, the target BNB side is $15,000, paired with approximately 50,000,000 FLOOR4 at the $0.00030 target DEX price. The target LP lock is 12 months. The exact DEX, pool type, lock provider and verification details remain to be finalized. The target pool price is not a guarantee of market price.

06 · Utility & ecosystem

Participation beyond the token

Planned ecosystem areas include community rewards in FLOOR4 and BNB, seasonal events, airdrops, games and community activities. The project has adopted two separate charity commitments: 0.4% of net realized project income after direct project costs, and a separate 0.4% earmark from the Treasury token allocation. At the current working Treasury allocation of 800,000,000 FLOOR4, the latter corresponds to 3,200,000 FLOOR4; the final token quantity depends on the final Treasury allocation. These are not fees on gross presale volume or individual transactions. The Christmas Challenge charity is a separate community-selected initiative. Staking is planned for future development; reward source, rate, lock-up, allocation and security design remain open. NFTs are a possible future feature, not a current product commitment.

07 · Community & governance

Community-led experiences

The project intends to use community participation for selected initiatives, including seasonal events and choosing a charitable purpose associated with the Christmas Challenge. A hybrid governance and voting model is planned for future development; its scope, voting weight and binding effect are not yet defined.

08 · Presale & launch planning

Planned presale window

The presale is planned for 24 November 2026 at 18:00 UTC to 4 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC. Payment is BNB only, the hard cap is $20,000 with no soft cap, the minimum purchase is $20 and the cumulative wallet limit is $400. The presale price remains unapproved and will not be published until formally decided. This window overlaps with the first four days of the planned Christmas Challenge, 1–4 December. The token launch is targeted for December 2026, but no specific launch date has been approved. Presale vesting is 20% at launch and 80% linearly over the following 90 days. Both presale and launch depend on technical, security, legal, financial and operational readiness.

No purchase functionality is enabled on this website preview. The final non-presale token distribution, contract address and verified participation instructions remain pending. An automatic transaction burn is an approved intention, but its rate, transaction scope and exemptions remain open.

09 · Roadmap

Seven development phases

The public roadmap is organized from Q4 2026 through Q4 2027: foundations; presale and token launch; Christmas Challenge; New Year and Lunar New Year activities; ecosystem expansion including planned staking; exploration of an additional centralized exchange target; and long-term ecosystem development.

These are planning windows, not guarantees. Exchange listings depend on independent third parties and are never assured.

10 · Risks & limitations

Understand the risks

Digital assets are volatile and may become illiquid or lose all value. Smart contracts can contain vulnerabilities; networks, wallets, exchanges and third-party services can fail or be compromised. Regulatory requirements may change and may restrict access in some jurisdictions. Roadmap items, utilities, events and exchange targets may be delayed, changed or cancelled.

Nothing in this draft guarantees profits, returns, liquidity, price appreciation, exchange listings or future token value. Do not commit funds you cannot afford to lose. Obtain independent legal, tax and financial advice where appropriate.
