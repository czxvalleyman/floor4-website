Christmas Challenge

24 days of discovery

The planned December advent calendar runs from 1–24 December and combines daily challenges, puzzles and community activities. The community will select the charitable purpose through a public vote. This event-specific charity is separate from the project’s ongoing charity commitments. The game is still being prepared and is not yet declared production-ready.

Future ecosystem

Help shape what comes next

Staking and a hybrid community governance model are areas of planned development. Staking rewards, lock-up terms and launch timing are not finalized. NFTs remain a possible future feature.

Project charity policy

Two separate commitments

The project has approved a contribution of 0.4% of net realized project income, plus a separate allocation equal to 0.4% of the final Treasury token allocation. These are distinct from the Christmas Challenge charity vote. The Treasury percentage and accounting implementation must follow the final approved token allocation and documented accounting rules.

Charity commitment

Giving is part of the project

FLOOR 4 has adopted two separate charity commitments: 0.4% of net realized project income after direct project costs, and a separate 0.4% earmark from the Treasury token allocation. The exact token amount depends on the final Treasury allocation, which remains a working proposal. This is not a fee on gross presale volume or every transaction. Recipients, accounting methodology and transparent reporting procedures must be established before distributions. The Christmas Challenge charity is separate and will be selected by the community.
